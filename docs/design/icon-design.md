# Icon design

The application icon was chosen among five concepts drawn in code. This document records the concepts, the
evaluation, the construction of the chosen one and the assets that ship.

All artwork is generated by `Scripts/icon.swift` with Core Graphics (`swift Scripts/icon.swift <outdir>`,
default `design/icon`). There is no hand-edited source file: the script is the source.

## Evaluation

The ratings below are a design judgement made on the rendered files at 1024, 320, 32 and 16 pixels. They are
not the result of a user study, and contrast was judged by eye, not measured.

| Criterion | 1. Ring sweep | 2. Treemap | 3. Disk sector | 4. Layers | 5. Monogram notch |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Originality | High: the segmented ring with a swept gap is specific to this product | Medium: block grids are common for disk tools and dashboards | Low: reads as a generic pie chart | Low: the stacked-layers glyph is widely used | Medium: a notched ring is distinctive but abstract |
| Recognisability (storage and cleaning) | High: used segments, then an emptied part | High for "storage map", weaker for "cleaning" | Medium: "a part removed", but also "chart" or "timer" | Low: reads as layers or a stack, not as disk space | Low: reads as a letter or a progress ring |
| Small-size readability | Medium: at 16 px the segments merge into a ring and the trail disappears; the gap and the spark remain | Medium: blocks hold, the dashed cell is lost | High: one shape, one cut | High: three flat shapes | High: one stroke and a dot |
| Balance | High: centred ring; the gap gives direction without tilting the mark | Medium: weight sits in the upper-left block | High: centred and symmetric apart from the cut | High: centred and symmetric | Medium: the dot pulls to the upper right |
| Brand consistency | High: the "O" of ORIX, in the teal-to-indigo family colours | Medium: family colours, no "O" | Medium: circular, single colour | Low: no link to the ORIX mark | High: the "O" itself, in the family gradient |
| Contrast on the dark plate | High: light teal, cyan and indigo segments, white spark | High: saturated blocks | Medium: one mid-tone blue; the cleared sector merges with the plate | Medium: translucent lower sheets | High: bright gradient stroke |

## Why concept 1 was chosen

- It is the only concept that carries all three ideas in one shape: the ORIX "O", storage in use (the
  segments) and the result of cleaning (the swept quarter).
- It shows the outcome, free space, rather than a tool or an alarm. This matches the tone of the product:
  factual, no urgency (see `brand-guidelines.md`).
- Concept 2 describes the Storage Map feature well but says "analysis" more than "reclaim", and its grid is the
  least distinctive silhouette next to other disk utilities.
- Concepts 3 and 4 are legible but generic.
- Concept 5 is the closest to a pure family monogram; it would suit a family mark but does not say what this
  application does.

Accepted weakness: concept 1 is the most detailed of the five and loses the most at 16 pixels. The ring, the
gap and the spark survive; the three separate segments and the trail do not.

## Construction

The canvas is 1024 by 1024 pixels, sRGB, with transparency. Coordinates below are in canvas pixels with the
origin at the bottom left, as in Core Graphics.

### Plate

| Element | Value |
|---|---|
| Shape | Rounded square, 824 by 824, origin (100, 100), centred on the canvas |
| Corner radius | 185.4 |
| Fill | Vertical gradient, `#1B2340` at the top to `#070A16` at the bottom |
| Drop shadow | Offset (0, -10), blur 22, black at 35 % |
| Glow | Radial gradient of `#2DD4BF`, 28 % at the centre (512, 540) to 0 % at radius 430, clipped to the plate |
| Edge highlight | 3-pixel stroke of white at 10 % along the plate outline, clipped to the plate |

The 824-pixel plate on a 1024 canvas leaves a 100-pixel margin on each side for the shadow, which is the usual
macOS application icon grid.

### Mark (concept 1)

Angles are in degrees, clockwise from 12 o'clock. The ring is centred at (512, 512) with radius 250.

| Element | Angles | Stroke width | Caps | Colours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Track | 0 to 360 | 20 | butt | white at 7 % |
| Segment 1 | 14 to 112 | 118 | round | `#5EEAD4` to `#14B8A6` |
| Segment 2 | 142 to 206 | 118 | round | `#22D3EE` to `#0891B2` |
| Segment 3 | 236 to 268 | 118 | round | `#818CF8` to `#5B5BF0` |
| Swept trail | ten dashes of 3.4 degrees starting at 290, one every 6.2 degrees | 20 | round | `#CCFBF1`, opacity rising from 16 % to 83.5 % |
| Spark | on the ring at -6 degrees | four-point star, half-size 62 | | white |
| Centre spark | (512, 512) | four-point star, half-size 30 | | `#5EEAD4` at 90 % |

The segments span 98, 64 and 32 degrees: each is shorter than the one before, so the ring reads as "emptying"
in the clockwise direction. Each segment is filled with a diagonal linear gradient across the bounding square
of the ring. The star is drawn with four quadratic curves whose control points sit at 22 % of its half-size,
which gives the concave sides.

## Assets generated

| File | Pixels | Use |
|---|---|---|

| `App/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/icon_1024.png` | 1024 | 512 pt @2x |
| `…/icon_512.png` | 512 | 512 pt @1x, 256 pt @2x |
| `…/icon_256.png` | 256 | 256 pt @1x, 128 pt @2x |
| `…/icon_128.png` | 128 | 128 pt @1x |
| `…/icon_64.png` | 64 | 32 pt @2x |

| `…/icon_16.png` | 16 | 16 pt @1x |
| `docs/assets/icon.png` | 256 | Documentation |
| `docs/assets/icon-512.png` | 512 | Documentation |

Seven PNG files fill the ten slots of the macOS `AppIcon` set (`Contents.json`). `Scripts/icon.swift` writes
only the 1024-pixel files; the step that produced the smaller sizes is not scripted in the repository, and the
small sizes are scaled versions of the same drawing, not separately simplified artwork.

The same plate colours and the tagline are reused on the disk-image background drawn by
`Scripts/dmg-background.swift` (660 by 420 points at 2x).

## What was not produced

- **No Icon Composer layered icon.** The layered format introduced for macOS 26 and later (separate layers
  with system-applied material, tint and appearance variants) was not produced. The application ships the
  classic asset-catalogue icon: flat PNGs with the plate, shadow and highlight baked in. How the system
  presents such an icon next to layered icons on macOS 26 and 27 has not been reviewed beyond the development
  machine.
- **No dark, tinted or clear variants.** There is one rendering.
- **No size-specific drawings.** A simplified 16- and 32-pixel version (ring, gap and spark only) would address
  the small-size weakness noted above.
- **No vector master** (SVG or PDF) other than the generator script.

## Regenerating

```sh
swift Scripts/icon.swift design/icon
```

This rewrites the five concepts and `ORIXClean-1024.png`. The asset-catalogue sizes and the two files in
`docs/assets/` must then be regenerated from `ORIXClean-1024.png` by hand.
